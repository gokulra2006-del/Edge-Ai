Electrical Level Invariant

Raspberry Pi GPIO pins are strictly 3.3V CMOS logic (not 5V tolerant). The 5V MQ-2 analog output is routed strictly to the ADS1115 ADC and never directly to a Raspberry Pi GPIO. The ADS1115 is powered from 3.3V, keeping its I2C lines safely clamped to 3.3V.

Shared I2C Bus Non-Collision

Both the GY-87 (MPU6050) and ADS1115 ADC share GPIO2 (SDA) and GPIO3 (SCL). Address collision is physically prevented: ADS1115 ADDR is tied to GND (0x48), while MPU6050 uses default address 0x68.

Power Isolation & Common Ground

High-draw inductive loads (SG90 servo motor & MQ-2 heater) have dedicated 5V power routing. All components share a common ground reference with the Raspberry Pi to eliminate ground loop voltage offsets.
